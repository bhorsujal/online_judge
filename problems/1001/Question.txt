

As a scientist confronting a virus outbreak, your mission is to devise a treatment strategy to mitigate the crisis.

After extensive analysis, you have formulated a treatment plan involving the injection of a specific quantity of anti-virus into the infected individual.

This quantity precisely matches the amount of virus present, ensuring effective neutralization.

Notably, you've observed that the anti-virus unit splits into three units when left overnight.

Given the initial virus load, your objective is to determine the minimum dosage of anti-virus required for treatment.


Input Format

The first line of input contains an integer t the number of test-cases.
The second line contains a single integer k the initial virus load.


Output Format

For each input value, output the amount of anti-virus units required for developing the treatment.


Constrainsts
1 <= t <= 10^4
0 <= k <= 10 ^9

Sample
2
9
11

output
1
3


Explanation

Case 1 : 1 unit of anti-virus left for 2 days will split into exactly 9 units.

Case 2 : Count of anti-virus units on day 0 -> 1
day 1 -> 3
day 2 -> 9
Adding 2 anti-virus units on day 2 will give the exact required amount of 11.
